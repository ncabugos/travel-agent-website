# EAH Curated Editorial Blueprint

The content engine behind the curated stream (Growth+). This defines the **pillars** (the durable
themes we publish against), the **categories** (the selectable taxonomy advisors toggle on their
sites), how each maps to **Virtuoso preferred suppliers**, and a **sample article bank** to seed the
blog.

Grounded in the [2026 Virtuoso Luxe Report](https://www.luxurytraveladvisor.com/data-trends/virtuosos-2026-luxe-report-maps-new-luxury-mindset)
and Virtuoso's [preferred supplier network](https://www.virtuoso.com/suppliers) (2,300+ hotels,
cruise lines, tour operators, and destinations).

---

## How the system works

The operator (you) writes against this blueprint. Each post is tagged to **one primary category**.
Advisors opt into categories via `agent_blog_preferences`, and only their selected categories push
to their site. So the taxonomy below is doing two jobs at once: it's our editorial calendar, and
it's the advisor's self-service filter.

**Design rule:** keep categories few, clean, and customer-facing. Pillars are internal strategy;
categories are what a client sees on the blog page. One post, one category, many tags.

**Cadence by tier:** Growth = 1 curated post/week. Custom = 2/week + topic requests. Agency = 2/week
per advisor with co-author option. Starter gets the editor but **not** the curated stream.

## The 2026 lens (why these themes win now)

The Luxe Report names five shifts. Every pillar below is built to ride at least one of them:

- **Crowd Control** — shoulder-season, lesser-known alternatives, private access.
- **Main Character Synergy** — personal, intentional, "designed around me" trips.
- **From FOMO to Slow-mo** — slow travel, longer stays, fewer stops.
- **Unlimited Luxe** — willingness to spend when it feels meaningful.
- **The Healthy Wealthy** — wellness as a primary motivator, now the #2 requested solo experience.

## Categories (the public taxonomy advisors select)

Ten categories. Each advisor turns on the ones that fit their niche. Recommended starting set is
marked. Each maps to a pillar and to the Virtuoso suppliers that give the content its credibility.

| Category | Pillar | Virtuoso suppliers in play | Good default for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Insider Access & Perks** | 1 | Virtuoso hotel program (breakfast, upgrade, ~$100 credit, late checkout) | Every advisor ✅ |
| **Destination Guides** | 2 | Destinations, DMCs, A&K, African Travel | Every advisor ✅ |
| **Hotels & Resorts** | 3 | Aman, Belmond, Four Seasons, Rosewood, Mandarin Oriental, One&Only, Auberge, Montage, Peninsula | Every advisor ✅ |
| **Cruise & Expedition** | 4 | Crystal, Riverside, Sea Cloud, Seabourn, Regent, expedition lines | Cruise-focused advisors |
| **River Cruise** | 4 | AmaWaterways, Avalon Waterways, Riverside | Europe / river specialists |
| **Wellness & Retreats** | 5 | Aman, COMO, Six Senses, Ayurvedic & spa resorts | Wellness advisors (e.g. Wine & Wellness) ✅ |
| **Food & Wine** | 5 / 2 | Belmond, culinary DMCs, wine-region properties | Culinary & wine advisors ✅ |
| **Family & Multigenerational** | 6 | Four Seasons, villas, A&K family journeys | Family-focused advisors |
| **Honeymoons & Celebrations** | 6 | Overwater & private-island resorts, villas | Romance / milestone advisors |
| **Safari & Adventure** | 6 / 2 | African Travel Inc., A&K, expedition operators | Adventure & safari advisors |

Notes:
- **"Insider Access & Perks" is the conversion category** — it's where we show what booking through a
  Virtuoso advisor actually gets you. Keep one fresh, evergreen anchor piece here at all times.
- **Villa content lives inside Family, Honeymoons, and Wellness** rather than its own public category,
  since villas are gated to Custom+ (per the Feature Matrix). Don't surface a "Villas" category on
  Starter/Growth UIs.
- Ten is the ceiling. Resist adding more — taxonomy bloat kills the advisor's filter UX.

## Editorial guardrails

- **Brand floor:** EAH is for the top 1% of advisors. Copy is invitation-worthy, never commodity.
  See `docs/brand-positioning.md` for voice and forbidden phrases.
- **No fabricated specifics:** perks, prices, and itineraries should reflect real Virtuoso program
  terms. When in doubt, generalize ("a property credit, typically around $100") rather than invent.
- **One category per post, tags for everything else.** A Tuscany wine-harvest piece is *Food & Wine*
  with tags for Italy, hotels, slow travel.
- **Production:** draft each post with the `eah-journal-writer` skill, which handles SEO + generative
  search formatting and the featured-image prompt workflow.
