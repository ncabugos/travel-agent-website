## 5. SEO/AEO strategy

### 5.1 Architecture: pillar pages + clusters

Build 4 pillar pages (2,500+ words, updated quarterly) with supporting cluster posts internally linked both ways:

**Cluster A — "Travel agent website" (commercial, P1/P2)**
Pillar: *The Complete Guide to Travel Advisor Websites (2026)*.
Supporting: best website builders for travel agents compared · travel agency website design: what luxury clients expect · how much does a travel agent website cost · travel advisor website examples · Squarespace for travel agents: where it breaks · what to put on a travel agent website.

**Cluster B — "Getting luxury travel clients" (TOFU, P1/P5)**
Pillar: *How Luxury Travel Advisors Get Clients in 2026*.
Supporting: travel agent marketing that respects the client · how clients vet an advisor before the first call · referral conversion (the site's real job) · do travel agents need a website (yes, and here is the data) · SEO for travel advisors · AI search: how clients now find advisors.

**Cluster C — "Virtuoso / independent advisor" (TOFU, P5)**
Pillar: *The Independent Luxury Advisor's Stack*.
Supporting: what Virtuoso affiliation actually gets you · host agency vs going independent · what is a Virtuoso travel advisor (client-facing query you can own) · the supplier relationships that define luxury advising.

**Cluster D — Comparisons & alternatives (BOFU, P2)**
Direct pages: EAH vs Squarespace · EAH vs hiring a web designer · EAH vs Wix · travel website template galleries reviewed. These are the pages answer engines cite for "best X for Y" queries; over 75% of B2B SaaS AI citations come from listicles, comparisons, and FAQ formats.

### 5.2 AEO standards (apply to every post)

1. **Answer-first:** open every post, and every H2 section, with a direct 40–60 word answer to the question in the heading. Then expand.
2. **Question-shaped H2s:** write headings as the literal queries ("How much does a travel advisor website cost?").
3. **FAQ block** at the end of every post (3–5 Q&As) with `FAQPage` schema. `Article` + `Person` (Nick) + `Organization` schema sitewide.
4. **E-E-A-T byline:** every post authored by Nick with a credentials line ("Virtuoso-affiliated advisor, founder of EAH"). Author page linking LinkedIn + Wine & Wellness Travel.
5. **Stats and named specifics:** answer engines favor content with statistics (~30% citation lift) and expert quotes (~41%). Nick's first-party observations count as expert sourcing.
6. **Freshness:** refresh pillar pages and top performers quarterly; pages stale >12 months lose AI citations at ~3x the rate. Put "Updated [Month Year]" on-page.
7. **Comparison tables** in every versus/cost post. LLMs extract tables cleanly.

### 5.3 Technical prerequisites (mostly already on the P1 list)

- A `/blog` or `/journal` surface on the eliteadvisorhub.com marketing site itself (distinct from advisor-site M18 journals). Needs: clean URLs, sitemap entries, RSS, schema. **This is the one build dependency this plan adds. Confirm scope before Week 1.**
- GA4 + PostHog funnel analytics (already P1), UTM discipline on every social link.
- Template showcase page and `/beta` waitlist page (already top recommendations in the beta strategy; every post CTA points at one of them).
- Google Business Profile claimed. Search Console verified, sitemap submitted.
- eliteadvisorhub.com models the SEO it sells: titles, metas, OG images per post.

## 8. The repurposing engine

One blog post becomes seven assets. Weekly batch, roughly 3 hours plus 15 min/day for engagement:

```
BLOG POST (Mon publish)
├─ LinkedIn text post: the post's sharpest claim + link (Tue)
├─ LinkedIn document post: 5–7 slide carousel of the framework (Thu)
├─ IG carousel: same frames, branded template (Wed)
├─ IG Reel: listicle version, on-screen text (Fri)
├─ X thread: condensed argument, 6–10 tweets (Tue)
├─ X singles: 2–3 standalone lines pulled from H2 answers (through week)
└─ FB page post: IG creative + blog link (Wed)
```

**Workflow (mirrors the Strategic Plan's queue-and-approve automation pattern):**

1. Monday 6am: journal pipeline drafts the week's post(s) (Automation Priority 1, already roadmapped for Month 2).
2. Nick approves/edits, publishes to the marketing blog.
3. Same session: Claude derives the social set above into a queue (this becomes Automation Priority 2, the IG engine, in Month 3).
4. Schedule via Buffer (or native schedulers) in one sitting. Engage 15 min/day.
5. Friday: log KPIs in the calendar's tracker sheet.
