[Showing up in AI search](https://www.eliteadvisorhub.com/insights/will-it-say-your-name) is only half the battle. The other half is what the AI actually says once you do. Right now, without your input, ChatGPT and Perplexity are answering client questions about your practice, and they are doing it with complete confidence whether or not they are correct. The question worth losing a little sleep over is not whether this is happening. It is whether the version of you the AI is handing your clients is the right one.

## Key takeaways
- AI already answers questions about you, confidently, accurate or not.
- A confident wrong answer reaches your client as fact, before you ever speak.
- In luxury, an inaccurate read is a reputation problem, not just a marketing one.
- You cannot correct AI in the conversation. You fix it by correcting the sources it reads.

## What it is saying when you are not in the room

Most advisors have never checked, and the results tend to surprise them. Sometimes the AI describes a specialty you left behind years ago. Sometimes it blends you with another advisor or agency. Sometimes it omits the very thing you are known for. And sometimes, asked for an advisor in your exact niche, it names a competitor and leaves you out entirely.

The unsettling part is the delivery. AI does not hedge. It states whatever it believes about you as plain fact, in a calm, authoritative voice that clients are inclined to trust. No "I think," no caveat. If the model holds an outdated or incomplete picture of you, it hands that picture to your client with total confidence.

## Why it gets you wrong

Because it assembles its answer from whatever it can find, and what it finds about you is often thin, scattered, or stale. When [your own site has not kept pace with who you are](https://www.eliteadvisorhub.com/insights/static-website-costs-you-clients), the engine fills the gaps with old mentions, secondhand listings, and inference. It is not malicious. It is working with poor source material and has no way to know the material is poor. This is why accuracy and visibility are really the same discipline: the engine reaches for [the clearest, most current, most consistent source about you](https://www.eliteadvisorhub.com/insights/geo-for-travel-advisors-get-cited), and it repeats whatever that source says.

## In luxury, this is reputation, not marketing

Your entire value rests on trust, taste, and the impression that you are precisely the right person for a particular kind of trip. A client doing early research who gets a muddled or inaccurate read on you does not call to clarify. They quietly move to whoever the AI described clearly and correctly. And the specific errors do real damage: being cast as a generalist when you are a specialist, associated with the wrong tier of travel, or left out of the exact category you have spent years building. That is the line between landing on a discerning client's shortlist and never being considered at all.

## How to fix the record

You cannot argue with the AI mid-conversation. You correct the sources it reads, starting with the one you own. Find out what each major engine says about you today, in the words it uses with a real client. Make your own website the authoritative, current, unambiguous account of who you are and whom you serve. Tighten the loose ends elsewhere so your identity is consistent across the web. Then keep checking, because these systems update and accuracy is maintained, not fixed once.
