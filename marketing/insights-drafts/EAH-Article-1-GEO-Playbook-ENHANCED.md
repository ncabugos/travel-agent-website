We have already made the case that [clients now ask AI to find and vet advisors](https://www.eliteadvisorhub.com/insights/will-it-say-your-name). The obvious next question is the one nobody answers: what do you actually do about it? Getting cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI is not luck and it is not a trick. It comes down to a handful of deliberate moves, and almost no advisor is making them yet.

## Key takeaways
- AI engines recommend the source that is clearest, most specific, most current, and most consistent about a given subject.
- Your single highest-leverage asset is one authoritative page that tells AI exactly who you are and what you do.
- Question-shaped, entity-rich content gets quoted. Vague brochure copy does not.
- This is maintenance, not a one-time project. The advisors who keep at it own the answer.

## Start with one page AI can trust

Before you publish anything new, fix the page that defines you. AI engines reach for the clearest source about who you are, and for most advisors that source is a thin, dated about page or, worse, a secondhand directory listing. Build one page that states plainly what you specialize in, the kind of trips you plan, the regions you know cold, and the clients you serve. Write it in direct language, not marketing fog. This is the page that teaches AI the right version of you, and everything else points back to it.

## Write the way AI reads

AI engines lift answers, so give them clean answers to lift. That means three habits. Lead each page with a direct response to the question it addresses, in the first few sentences, before any throat-clearing. Phrase your headings as the questions a client would actually ask, because that is how the engine matches your content to their query. And be relentlessly specific: name the regions, the properties, the experiences, the trip types. "Stunning destinations" gets ignored. "Private barrel tastings in Napa for milestone trips" gets quoted.

## Be the same advisor everywhere

AI builds its picture of you from across the web, and contradictions make it hesitate. If your specialty reads one way on your site, another in a directory, and a third on a profile you forgot about, the engine has no confident answer to give, so it gives someone else's. Make your identity consistent wherever it appears: the same specialty, the same description, the same name. Consistency is a trust signal, and trust is what earns the citation. It is also how you keep AI from [handing clients an inaccurate version of you](https://www.eliteadvisorhub.com/insights/is-ai-describing-your-practice-accurately).

## Keep it alive

This is the part most advisors miss. AI favors current content and quietly discounts pages that have not moved in months. A site you built once and walked away from [fades out of the answers](https://www.eliteadvisorhub.com/insights/static-website-costs-you-clients), no matter how good it was on launch day. Publishing on your specialty on a steady cadence is not busywork. It is the signal that tells the engine you are an active, current authority worth recommending. The advisor who keeps showing up beats the one who showed up once.

## Show your proof

AI weighs credibility, and it reads the same trust signals a client does. Real specifics about your practice, the recognitions that matter in this field, the clear sense that you are an established professional rather than a placeholder. You do not need to manufacture this. You need to make sure it is present and legible on the page, not buried or assumed.

## The short version

Build one page AI can trust. Write in clear, specific, question-shaped language. Stay consistent everywhere your name appears. Keep publishing. Show your proof. None of it requires you to become a technologist. It requires treating your online presence as the thing that recommends you before you ever speak, because that is now exactly its job.

**What is GEO for travel advisors?**
GEO, or generative engine optimization, is the practice of structuring your online presence so AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI recommend you by name. For advisors it comes down to four moves: one authoritative page, specific question-shaped content, a consistent identity across the web, and steady publishing.

**How do I get ChatGPT to recommend me to clients?**
Build one clear page stating your specialty, your regions, and the clients you serve. Write in specific, question-shaped language the engine can quote. Keep your identity identical across every directory and profile. Then publish on your specialty regularly. AI cites the clearest, most current, most consistent source it can find.

**How often should I publish to stay visible in AI search?**
A steady cadence matters more than volume. Publishing something substantive on your specialty even twice a month signals to AI engines that you are an active, current authority. Pages that sit still for months get quietly discounted, no matter how strong they were at launch.
