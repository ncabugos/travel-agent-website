Brand style guide

Elite Advisor Hub

Websites for luxury travel advisors. This guide covers the marketing site, the admin and advisor portals' marketing surfaces, and email.

Revised September 2026

Principles

Simplicity is sophistication

One idea per screen. Remove before adding.

One accent per screen

Purple appears once, on the primary action.

Imagery does the work

Full-bleed supplier photography with a flat dark overlay. No decorative graphics.

Sentence case, plain words

No title case, no exclamation marks, no metaphor. Voice rules live in docs/brand-positioning.md.

Show the specifics

Name the hotel programs, the cruise lines, the numbers: 24 preferred-partner programs, 1,805 luxury hotels, 103 countries, 28 cruise lines as of September 2026.

Typography

Inter Tight for display, Inter for body and UI. Two families, five weights in total. Display type is light and tightly tracked; body type is regular and generously leaded.

Shape and surfaces

Corners

0 on images and containers. 2px on buttons and inputs. Nothing rounder.

Separation

1px hairlines in Divider separate content. Never a box with a border plus a shadow. No shadows at all.

Dark sections

Near black #0B0A09, with photography at 50 to 60 percent under a flat rgba(11,10,9,0.6) overlay. Text is white, secondary text is white at 72 percent.

Light sections

Alternate white and cream. The change of ground is the only section boundary you need.

Text link

  • Underline rises to full color on hover
  • Inter 15px, weight 500
  • Never colored purple

Imagery

Use full-bleed supplier photography from the catalog: hotels, ships, villas, landscapes. Choose frames with a clear subject and open space for type. Prefer architecture and place over people.

Apply a flat rgba(11,10,9,0.6) overlay and set type in white. No gradients, no blur, no tint. Never place a logo, badge, or illustration on top of a photograph.

Email

One shell for every message: auth, system notices, founder letters. Cream ground, a white square card with a hairline border, the 160px logo, a 40px gold rule, Inter throughout, a flat purple 2px button, and a 12px footer. Nothing else is added per email.
