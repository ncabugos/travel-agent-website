# Featured Image Prompts — June/July 2026 Curated Batch

Two ways to source the hero for each article:

1. **Pull from the supplier media library first.** Each article's `hero_image_brief` (in its frontmatter)
   doubles as a search brief. Use the suggested **library search terms** below against the Virtuoso /
   supplier media database or your licensed stock library (the photo brief avoids AI-style tokens on
   purpose, because licensed photography is the house standard). Pull licensed imagery whenever a clean
   match exists — it always beats generated art for a luxury audience.
2. **Generate only as fallback.** If no licensed shot fits, use the **ChatGPT/DALL·E prompt** below.
   All prompts are 16:9, photographic, editorial, and free of text, logos, and recognizable faces.

A note on the brand floor: keep heroes restrained and architectural, not "stock-photo luxury." No
champagne-on-a-beach clichés, no over-saturated sunsets, no smiling models to camera.

---

## 1. Is a Luxury Travel Advisor Worth It? (Travel Intelligence)

**Library search terms:** travel advisor desk, open leather folio, marked map, fountain pen, espresso, considered workspace, editorial still life.

**Generate prompt:**
> Editorial still-life photograph, 16:9. An open leather travel folio on a wooden desk at eye level, beside a marked-up paper map of a coastline, a fountain pen resting on it, and a single espresso cup. Soft natural window light from the left, shallow depth of field, warm muted tones. No laptop or screen visible, no people, no text or logos. Quiet and considered mood, like a Monocle or Departures feature opener. Realistic photography, not illustration.

## 2. Where to Go When: A Shoulder-Season Map for 2026 (Seasonal Planning)

**Library search terms:** quiet Italian hill town piazza, off-season Mediterranean village, empty café terrace autumn light, low-season Europe, October light cobblestones.

**Generate prompt:**
> Travel photograph, 16:9. A near-empty stone piazza in a small Italian hill town in low October light — long shadows, warm low-angle sun, a single café with two occupied outdoor tables. The emptiness is the subject: shoulder season, not high summer. Generic Mediterranean architecture, no recognizable landmark, no signage, no crowds. Natural color, gentle contrast, editorial travel-magazine style. Realistic photography.

## 3. A Second Trip to Japan: Beyond Tokyo and Kyoto (Destinations)

**Library search terms:** ryokan engawa veranda, moss garden rain, traditional Japanese inn, slippers at threshold, dark timber, onsen steam, rural Japan.

**Generate prompt:**
> Atmospheric travel photograph, 16:9. The wooden engawa veranda of a traditional Japanese ryokan opening onto a moss garden in light rain, a single pair of slippers at the step, faint steam rising from an unseen bath. Muted greens and dark timber, soft overcast light, shallow depth of field. No people, no signage, no text. Quiet arrival mood, like an AFAR or Condé Nast Traveler feature. Realistic photography, not illustration.

## 4. Small-Ship vs. Mega-Ship: How to Choose a Luxury Cruise (Cruise Guides)

**Library search terms:** small luxury ship teak deck, empty cruise deck dawn, ocean horizon railing, intimate yacht deck, calm sea morning light.

**Generate prompt:**
> Travel photograph, 16:9. The teak deck of a small luxury cruise ship at sea in early-morning light, polished railings in the foreground and an empty open horizon beyond — no other ships, no crowds, a couple of unoccupied lounge chairs. Intimate, yacht-like scale, not a multi-pool mega-ship. Cool dawn palette, soft light, calm water. No people, no text or branding. Realistic editorial photography.

## 5. Aman Tokyo: Quiet Luxury Above the Otemachi Towers (Hotel Spotlights)

**Library search terms:** Aman Tokyo lobby atrium, washi paper stone interior, soaring hotel atrium daylight, minimalist Japanese luxury interior, ikebana, architectural calm.

> **Try the supplier/property media library first for this one** — Aman provides press imagery of the
> Otemachi atrium and rooms, which is the ideal hero and avoids any accuracy risk in a generated
> interior.

**Generate prompt (fallback only):**
> Architectural interior photograph, 16:9. A soaring hotel lobby atrium of washi paper, stone, and dark timber inside a skyscraper, daylight washing down from far above, a single low ikebana arrangement in the foreground. Emphasis on scale, material, and calm — paper and stone, not glitz or gold. Almost empty of people, no text or logos. Muted natural palette, serene mood. Realistic photography, not a recognizable real-property reproduction.
