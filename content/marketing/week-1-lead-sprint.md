# Week 1 Lead Sprint

> Built from your real data: HubSpot sends + GA4, June 2026. Action-first.
> Date: June 23, 2026

---

## What the data says (the unlock)

**Email.** 436 cold emails across 4 blasts (228, 46, 108, 54). 0% click-through on every single one. Delivery 70% to 88%, which means up to 30% bounced. That bounce rate is actively damaging your sending domain's reputation.

**Site traffic.** 728 sessions in 28 days, but 494 of them (68%) are "Direct" with 7-second engagement. That is mostly bots and your own visits. Ignore it. Your real, engaged traffic is **Organic Search: 109 sessions, 64% engagement, 1 minute 34 seconds average.** That is genuinely good, and it is already arriving without you spending a cent.

**Conversions.** 0 key events in GA4. 0 leads in Supabase. Every interested visitor leaks away.

**The conclusion:** your bottleneck is capture and targeting, not volume. Sending more bulk email to cold lists will keep getting 0% clicks while it poisons your deliverability. The fix is to capture the interest you already create, and to reach the right people one to one.

---

## Stop doing (today)

Stop the bulk cold blasts through HubSpot to imported lists. A 12% to 30% bounce rate will wreck deliverability for all your email, including the personal 1:1 notes you are about to start sending.

---

## Do this week, in order

### Day 1: Build a capture path that records leads (no engineering)

1. Turn on **HubSpot Meetings** (free). Create a "20-minute walkthrough" booking link. Bookings auto-log to your CRM.
2. Create one **HubSpot form**: "See a live agency site." Fields: name, agency, email, number of advisors.
3. These two become the destination for every email, DM, and on-site CTA. Now interest is captured and tracked instead of vanishing.

### Day 1: Fix tracking so leads are visible

4. In GA4, mark "form submit" and "meeting booked" as **Key Events**.
5. Wire the website consultation form into HubSpot. Right now it only writes to Supabase, which is empty, so even real leads would be invisible to your outreach.

### Day 2 to 3: Switch to 1:1 high-touch outreach (your ASAP lever)

6. Pick **15 to 20 hand-picked boutique agencies**, not hundreds.
7. Each one gets a LinkedIn connection with a short note, then a personalized 1:1 email from your own mailbox (not HubSpot marketing), pointing to a **live site example plus your booking link.**
8. Twelve genuinely personal touches will beat 228 blasts. Target: 3 to 5 booked walkthroughs this week.

### Day 2: Protect deliverability

9. Verify SPF, DKIM, and DMARC on your sending domain.
10. Send 1:1 outreach from a normal Gmail or Workspace mailbox, not bulk marketing email.
11. Remove every address that has bounced from your lists.

### Day 3 to 5: Plug the organic leak

12. Add one clear CTA to your highest-traffic pages and every blog post: "See a live advisor site" or "Book a walkthrough," pointing to the capture path. Your organic-search visitors are your best audience and they currently hit a dead end.

---

## What to expect (honest)

- **1:1 outreach:** first booked calls within days if the targeting and personalization are right.
- **Organic capture:** a trickle now (real organic is roughly 4 quality sessions a day), compounding over weeks once the CTA and lead magnet exist.
- **Content and SEO:** weeks to months. Real, but not the ASAP lever. Do not wait on it for this week's leads.

---

## The one move that matters most

Capture plus 1:1 outreach. Build the booking path today, then personally reach 20 right-fit agencies this week, each with a live-site link. Everything else supports those two.
