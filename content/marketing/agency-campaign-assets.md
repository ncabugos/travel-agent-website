## H. HubSpot setup (the missing plumbing)

The order of operations to make all of the above trackable:

1. **Reauthorize HubSpot** with write and marketing scopes so forms, workflows, and campaigns are usable.
2. **Wire the website forms into HubSpot** so consultation and inquiry submissions create contacts, instead of sitting only in Supabase.
3. **Build a deal pipeline** with stages: New Lead, Contacted, Walkthrough Booked, Trial Started, Won, Lost. Every replied-to outreach becomes a deal.
4. **Create a capture form** for the lead magnet, connected to a list.
5. **Build two workflows:** one that enrolls magnet downloaders into the nurture sequence, and one that creates a follow-up task for you whenever someone replies or books.
6. **Tag your imported list** as Cold Outbound so it never gets mixed with inbound, and measure the two separately.

Once this exists, the next time you ask "why am I not getting leads," the answer will be a number on a chart instead of a guess.
