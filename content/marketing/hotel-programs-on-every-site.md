# The Hotel Programs Your Clients Should See on Your Site

A client comparing your quote to a booking site rarely sees the difference. The rate looks the same. What they cannot see, until you show them, is that booking through you means a confirmed suite upgrade at The Dorchester, a daily breakfast for two at Rosewood, and a resort credit at Four Seasons that the public rate will never carry.

That gap is where bookings are won and lost. Every Elite Advisor Hub site closes it with a Hotel Programs module that presents the elite partner programs you can book, in language and design your clients understand at a glance. It ships on every tier, from Starter up, and the operator keeps the catalog current so you never explain a benefit that quietly changed last quarter.

## What the Hotel Programs module actually does

Most advisor websites bury supplier value in a paragraph on an About page. Yours puts it on the table. Each program gets its own presentation: the brand mark, editorial photography, a plain statement of the benefits, and the recognition your clients receive at the door. A guest browsing your site sees the same upgrade, credit, and breakfast they would get, attached to a name they already trust.

The point is not decoration. It is proof. When a client can see that a Belmond stay booked through you includes a complimentary upgrade, daily breakfast for two, and up to a $200 hotel credit, the conversation stops being about price. It becomes about access, which is the only ground where an advisor wins.

## Which programs are showcased?

The catalog spans the invitation-only programs that define luxury hospitality. A selection of what your clients will recognize:

**Four Seasons Preferred Partner.** Complimentary breakfast, often available as room service at no charge, a resort or spa credit, the highest upgrade priority available on any platform, and flexible arrival and departure. No minimum stay, so the benefits hold on a single night.

**Belmond Bellini Club.** Recognition across Belmond's hotels, legendary trains, and river journeys: an upgrade on arrival, daily breakfast for two, and up to a $200 flexible property credit, with VIP welcome and Bellini-only offers that never reach the public.

**Rosewood Elite.** Breakfast for two, a room or suite upgrade, a $100 hotel credit, and a welcome amenity, with the distinct advantage that the perks combine with most Rosewood promotional rates, including the fourth-night-free offers your clients want stacked.

**The Dorchester Collection Diamond Club.** A guaranteed one-tier upgrade confirmed at the time of booking, not left to chance at check-in, full daily breakfast including in-room dining, a property credit, and a 24/7 Diamond Club Ambassador team on call throughout the stay.

**Mandarin Oriental Fan Club.** Daily breakfast for two, an upgrade on arrival, and a guest's choice of a $100 food and beverage or spa credit, a flexibility most programs do not offer, plus priority upgrades over competing programs.

**Kempinski Club 1897.** Rates exclusive to trusted partners, with daily breakfast for two, a room upgrade, a $50 to $100 hotel credit depending on property, and automatic placement at the top of the upgrade list, above guests who booked direct or through a card concierge.

**Marriott STARS and Luminous.** STARS covers the ultra-luxury houses, including Ritz-Carlton STARS, St. Regis, EDITION, Bvlgari, and The Luxury Collection. Luminous covers premium and lifestyle brands like W, JW Marriott, and Westin. Both deliver daily breakfast for two, a $100 hotel credit, upgrades on availability, and a welcome amenity, while still earning your clients their points and elite nights.

**Hyatt Privé.** Exclusive rates not bookable by the public, daily breakfast for two, a $100 property credit, and flexible check-in and check-out across Park Hyatt, Andaz, Alila, and Miraval, with points and qualifying nights intact.

**Jumeirah Passport to Luxury.** A room upgrade on arrival, daily breakfast for two, a $100 resort credit with an additional $50 per night from the third night onward, a complimentary transfer at many properties, and a four o'clock late check-out.

**Shangri-La Luxury Circle.** The partner program above the standard loyalty tier: complimentary breakfast, a room upgrade, and a property credit at Shangri-La hotels across Asia and beyond, bookable only through an affiliated advisor.

Presented together on your site, these programs do something a quote never can. They make the scale of what you offer visible.

## Why "always up to date" is the part that matters

Hotel programs change. A credit moves from $100 to $150. A brand joins STARS. A benefit gets a new condition. When that happens on a self-built site, the advisor is the last to know, and a client eventually catches the discrepancy at check-in. That is the worst possible place to be wrong.

Elite Advisor Hub maintains the program catalog centrally. The operator tracks the benefit sheets and updates the shared catalog, and the change flows to your site. You are never the one quoting a credit that ended in March. The catalog depth and its upkeep are the real moat here, and they are not work you should be doing between client trips.
